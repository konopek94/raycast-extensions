# Raycastini Timers Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Natural-language timers and reminders: `25m tea`, `at 17:00 call mom`, `tomorrow 10am dentist`, `fri 18:00 call dad`
- Repeating: `every 1h stretch`, `daily 9am meds`, `weekdays 9am standup`, `every mon, wed 18:00 gym`
- Ringing screen in Raycast with Done / Snooze; sound repeats until dismissed
- Pause and resume countdown timers
- Recent timers and configurable presets
- Menu bar countdown
- Timers are re-armed after logout or reboot
